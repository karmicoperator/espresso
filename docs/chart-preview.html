What the trial found

The headline is one number, and it means nothing on its own. 0.83 is a rate ratio, age-adjusted, for death within 28 days, with an interval that clears 1. The paper says all of that in one sentence.

So the sentence is the chart.

Rate ratio, 28-day mortality

Age-adjusted, all randomised patients

0.83
Mortality at 28 days was significantly lower in the dexamethasone group than in the usual care group, with deaths reported in 482 of 2104 patients (22.9%) and in 1110 of 4321 patients (25.7%), respectively (rate ratio, 0.83; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.75 to 0.93) — Results, primary outcome
